How this is measured. Every job carries the crew's walkthrough estimate (the hours the movers commit to on site) and the total billable time (what it actually took). An over-run = the job ran at least 30 minutes past the estimate. Per your call, this tracks over-runs only — jobs that blew past what the customer was told — and attributes each over-run to the whole crew on the job. A mover's over-run rate = share of their jobs that ran long (minimum 5 jobs to rank). "Hours long" = total hours over estimate across those jobs. The worst 25% of each branch's rated movers is the coaching priority.
