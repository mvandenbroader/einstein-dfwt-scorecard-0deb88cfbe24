Estimate-to-Actual

Source: branch contract sheets (Move Manager "Input" tab) · window Aug 1–9, 2026 (Q3-to-date, today excluded) · refreshes forward each cycle · the crew-accountability side of the time equation
How this is measured. Every job carries the crew's walkthrough estimate (the hours the movers commit to on site) and the total billable time (what it actually took). An over-run = the job ran at least 30 minutes past the estimate. Per your call, this tracks over-runs only — jobs that blew past what the customer was told — and attributes each over-run to the whole crew on the job. A mover's over-run rate = share of their jobs that ran long (minimum 5 jobs to rank). "Hours long" = total hours over estimate across those jobs. The worst 25% of each branch's rated movers is the coaching priority.

Region — over-run rate by branch

Notes: "Estimate" is the movers' on-site walkthrough number, not the sales quote — it's the crew's own commitment, which is why the crew owns the miss. A high over-run rate means the crew consistently takes longer than they told the customer: OT, schedule cascade, and a bill bigger than the estimate. Coming in under estimate is not flagged here (your call — over-runs only). The sheet also tracks estimated vs actual men, so when actual crew > estimated crew, support was pulled in — a separate signal we can layer later. · Feeds the Critical Coaching compounding view. · Part of the Road to A+ Command Center.
