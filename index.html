The morning refresh

Same workflow as the sales dashboard: once you're signed into EMMA in the morning, Gerald refreshes. Runs as often as you like (EMMA logs out overnight, so it needs your fresh sign-in first). The weekly lens is just the review layer on top — trends and coaching aim.

  1. You sign into EMMA — the EMMA-fed tiles (TUR, OT, branch + individual sales) can only pull once your session is live for the day.
  2. Gerald refreshes — auto-pulls Games, Praise, damages + driver-safety offenders, QG pace (Monday + Samsara + dashboard reads), then pulls the EMMA numbers.
  3. Hiring / staffing — the tile is FTE vs the branch's 2-week block goal from the Q3 TUR projection (Table 2). Same source and block schedule that drives the Hiring Goal Tracker, so the two never diverge. Goals step down into the slow season on their own as the weeks cross blocks.
  4. Trend + flag — each tile gets an arrow vs the prior read; any branch drifting toward a Red gets flagged.
  5. Aim the week — the flags become your one-on-one and site-visit targets. The /command skill runs the refresh one metric at a time: /command sales · praise · hiring · games · damages · driving · estimate · OT (or /command all to run them in order).
Customer NPS — by design, no tile. NPS is the output, not an input. It's driven almost entirely by damages and estimate-to-actual. Coach the low performers there up or out and NPS follows, so we track the two causes, not the symptom. (It's shown inside each branch's QG breakdown.)
Gross margin — monthly. Wires in as a per-branch line when the monthly gross-margin meetings start and the spreadsheets exist. Margin is a monthly close, not a weekly number.
