How to read this for the branches

  • Dallas is still the OT story (17.7%), but it's moving the right direction — down from 22.6%. Five movers are on chronic 2-week OT; those schedules are the place to work.
  • Fort Worth needs an immediate look. It jumped from 5.6% to 13.4% in one week, and it's two names — Kenan Harris (38h) and Elijah Rasberry (28h). A targeted fix, not a systemic one.
  • McKinney fixed its OT — 12.5% down to 3.9%. Worth acknowledging with the branch.
  • OT ties straight to NOI. Every point of OT rate is margin off the branch's bottom line — the most controllable weekly cost lever on the board.
