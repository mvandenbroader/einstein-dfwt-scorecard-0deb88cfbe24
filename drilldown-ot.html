How to read this for the branches

  • Dallas is the OT story. 2,662 OT hours in Q2 — more than the other four branches combined (1,518). It ran 300+ OT hours a week for much of May–June. On the lowest-margin branch in the region, that's the clearest single cost lever to attack.
  • Tampa's low OT isn't a win. 4.3% looks great, but it's low because trucks sit idle — it's the utilization problem showing up from another angle, not efficient scheduling.
  • McKinney is genuinely lean on OT (5.3%) despite its other issues — worth acknowledging.
  • OT ties straight to NOI. This feeds the Phase 3 NOI leading-indicators view: revenue pace, OT, damages, and TUR together predict the monthly NOI close.
