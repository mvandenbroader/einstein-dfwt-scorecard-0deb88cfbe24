How to read this for coaching

  • Start with Critical Coaching Required. The drivers who are worst-25% in more than one category are the top priority, ahead of anyone who's a single-category issue. (That page refreshes this cycle once estimate + OT are pulled for the same window.)
  • Driving ≠ handling. A bad damages mover can be a perfectly safe driver (100 score) — so they don't compound, and they're a training problem, not a discipline one. Don't assume a bad mover is a bad driver.
  • Fort Worth and Tampa prove it's coachable. Zero safety offenders on either. Their driver-coaching cadence is the model for Dallas and McKinney.
